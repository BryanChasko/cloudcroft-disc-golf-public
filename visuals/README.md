# visuals

curated copies of already-published images, downloaded byte-identical from the live site. no new media was generated for this folder.

- `map-hero.webp` — 2d map view of both courses, as published at https://bryanchasko.com/cloudcroft-disc-golf/ (source: `story/assets/map-hero.webp`).
- `walkthrough-hero.webp` — 3d orbit view over the 1 m DEM terrain, as published at https://bryanchasko.com/cloudcroft-disc-golf/ (source: `story/assets/walkthrough-hero.webp`).
- `kiosk.webp` — course kiosk photo from the november 1 2026 ground survey, as published on the story page (source: `story/assets/kiosk.webp`).
- `hole2-tee-sign.webp` — community hole 2 tee-sign photo from the november 1 2026 ground survey, as published on the story page (source: `story/assets/hole2-tee-sign.webp`).
- `fig-swmaps-community-tee2.webp` — SW Maps recording screenshot for community tee 2 (par, distance, mando note), november 1 2026 survey, as published on the story page (source: `story/assets/fig-swmaps-community-tee2.webp`).
