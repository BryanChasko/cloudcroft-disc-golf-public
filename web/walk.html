For the judges

Dataset
USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM + NAIP 2022 + OpenStreetMap trails + a Nov 1 ground GPS survey (21 community points, 6 tiki points, 5 panos). Every pin is an averaged fix, nothing fabricated.
Map + services
Amazon Location Service tiles, Rust ARM lambdas, DynamoDB scorecard, this 1 m-terrain Babylon walkthrough.
Pattern to reuse
Walk → averaged points with remarks → GeoJSON → validator → playable card. The next survey drop lights new holes with zero code changes.
